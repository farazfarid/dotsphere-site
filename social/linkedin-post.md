## English

I just released Dotsphere: 48 animated dotted status orbs for SwiftUI, open source under MIT.

It started with CarPlay in my navigation app Leadwolf, where the AI guide's orb needed to look better. I found thinking-orbs by Jakub Antalik, a web library for exactly this kind of status indicator, and translated it line by line to Swift. The first nine states compute exactly like the original, checked against his reference data. Then I added 39 states of my own: an eye, a heartbeat, an hourglass, a coin toss, fireworks, a DNA helix and more.

What's inside:
• 48 states, pure Swift, no assets, no dependencies
• iOS, macOS, tvOS, watchOS and visionOS
• Colour, density and size as parameters
• A renderer for places where a live view can't go: CarPlay, widgets, Live Activities

The website doubles as a playground. Scroll through all 48 live and tune them in an iPhone mockup.

→ dotsphere.farazfarid.com
→ github.com/farazfarid/dotsphere

Big thanks to Jakub Antalik for the original. If you build for the web, check out thinking-orbs. Dotsphere is an unofficial Swift port.
